Memory Without Curation:
Learning Multi-Stream Latent Memory for Robot Policies

Yilin Wu1,2,† Rutav Shah1,3 Shuaiyi Huang1 Sumedh A. Sontakke1 Homanga Bharadhwaj1,4 Andrea Bajcsy2 Max Simchowitz1,2 Guanya Shi1,2
1Amazon FAR 2Carnegie Mellon University 3The University of Texas at Austin 4Johns Hopkins University †Work done during the internship at Amazon FAR

Approach

Method

IMPRINT compresses visual, action, and proprioceptive history into a fixed-size latent memory, bootstraps that memory with reconstruction and task-progress supervision, and then jointly adapts it with a VLA for control.

Experiments

Result Highlights

Contributions

  1. 1

    We propose IMPRINT, a policy-agnostic latent memory framework that efficiently compresses multi-stream histories and trains with history reconstruction and progress estimation before joint fine-tuning with the policy.

  2. 2

    We systematically analyze how memory capacity, encoding, training objectives, and training recipes affect downstream performance.

  3. 3

    Across RMBench, a 12-task RoboCasa benchmark, and three real-world tasks, IMPRINT improves success over the base VLA by 47.4% on RMBench and 50% in the real world, while achieving the lowest average latency and highest success among the memory baselines in the latency comparison.
